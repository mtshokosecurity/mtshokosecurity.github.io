Is Mtshoko Security registered with PSIRA?

Yes. Mtshoko Security and Training Services (Reg. No. 2013/032181/07) is a registered security service provider under the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority, PSIRA registration number 2337155.

Which areas of South Africa do you cover?

We render security services in all nine provinces: Gauteng, Eastern Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, North West, Mpumalanga, Western Cape, Northern Cape, Limpopo and Free State.

Do you provide armed and unarmed guards?

Yes. We provide 24 hour armed and unarmed guards, matched to the risk profile and requirements of your site.

What security services do you offer besides guarding?

We also offer VIP protection, secure escorts, event security, bouncers and 24/7 CCTV monitoring.
